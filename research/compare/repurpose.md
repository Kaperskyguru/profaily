#### Repurpose.io facts (support.repurpose.io and repurpose.io/pricing, checked November 19, 2026)
Carried over from the pre-agent research notes. Re-verify each line on the next audit of this page.
- Model: workflows from a source to destinations. Sources include TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, Facebook, Google Drive, Dropbox, Zoom, Webex, HeyGen, Twitch, podcast RSS. (Supported platforms, roles and media types)
- No direct upload from your device is described; Drive and Dropbox are the closest.
- Auto publish: turn it on for a workflow and it publishes existing and future videos. (Automatically repurpose TikTok videos)
- Pulls the original video before TikTok or Instagram add a watermark; a watermark already in the file can't be removed.
- TikTok source: Public videos only; TikTok Drafts not supported as a source; TikTok up to 10 min, 20 uploads a day per account.
- YouTube Shorts: square or vertical, up to 3 min; in auto mode longer videos are skipped; optional auto trim of the end.
- Failed-job reasons viewable on web, and on mobile since the 22 Jun 2026 release. Republish button.
- Calendar month, week and day; edit a scheduled post's title, description, date and time.
- One workflow per platform; each copy scheduled for a date and time or auto-published in time slots.
- Video templates (9:16, 1:1), burn-in captions, clips from long-form; manual trimmer for Shorts.
