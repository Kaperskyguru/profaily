#### Repurpose.io facts (support.repurpose.io and repurpose.io, checked November 21, 2026)
Help-center dates are DD/MM/YYYY.
- Workflows from a source to destinations, one per platform: "You will need to create a new workflow for each Social Platform". (support.repurpose.io/en/article/how-to-publish-webex-recordings-to-all-your-social-platforms-97k4hp/, 15/03/2026)
- Same-platform workflows can no longer be created (22 Jun 2026 release). (release notes, page updated 05/10/2026)
- No device upload: "Repurpose does not currently support directly uploading video or media files"; use Google Drive or Dropbox. (/en/article/how-scheduling-works-in-repurpose-setup-and-limits-iyhn5d/, 13/09/2026)
- Source and destination: TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, Snapchat, YouTube, Google Drive, Dropbox; more sources and destinations listed. (/en/article/supported-platforms-roles-and-media-types-j22278/, 28/09/2026)
- YouTube: videos and Shorts. (same, 28/09/2026)
- Manual scheduling: Schedule next to a post, pick a date and time. (how-scheduling-works, 13/09/2026)
- Auto publish: as soon as possible or on a schedule; up to 5 slots a day. (/en/article/how-auto-publishing-works-in-repurpose-aktmnm/, 28/07/2026)
- Calendar: monthly, weekly and daily; edit headline and description, reschedule date and time. (/en/article/calendar-view-114r1jj/, 01/07/2025)
- YouTube Shorts: up to 180 s; in auto mode longer videos are skipped, optional auto trim; skipped videos can be published or scheduled manually with a trimmer. (/en/article/youtube-shorts-60-seconds-vs-3-minutes-5gv5cv/, 29/09/2026)
- Images: Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, LinkedIn, Pinterest, X destinations; Drive folders publish as carousels; YouTube not an image destination. (/en/article/image-support-for-posts-and-stories-knx7ju/, 18/11/2026)
- Facebook: Business Pages only; publishes to Reels, Stories and Images. (/en/article/how-to-connect-your-facebook-account-in-repurpose-e833we/, 20/07/2026; /en/article/facebook-connection-faq-troubleshooting-guide-40b3ec/, 21/07/2026)
- TikTok source: public videos only, TikTok Drafts not a source; TikTok destination can send to TikTok's own drafts (28 Sep 2026 release).
- Failed-job reasons on web and mobile (22 Jun 2026 release); Republish button. (/en/article/how-do-i-post-videos-longer-than-15mins-on-youtube-1vog5ol/)
- Failure email: "An email is sent each time a process fails." (25 Aug 2021 release). "Email notifications on failed jobs are now disabled by default (you can re-enable them in settings)." (14 May 2026 release)
- Captions for Drive/Dropbox files from a TXT file, Drive description, or file name; optional AI rewrite. No caption variables (only {{episode-link}}, per episode). (/en/article/how-to-add-description-and-timestamps-to-google-drive-and-dropbox-files-s1t4go/, 20/11/2026)
- No drafts or labels inside Repurpose; ways to keep videos out of auto publish are filters and manual mode. (/en/article/how-to-prevent-specific-videos-from-being-auto-published-1vmt3v6/, 08/06/2026)
- Creator Invite Link approves connected accounts, not posts (28 Sep 2026 release).
- No team members, roles, post approvals, per-brand access, MCP or AI campaign planning on its pages.

## Prices (repurpose.io/pricing, checked November 21, 2026)
- Billed in USD. Flat price per plan; limits per network.
- Starter: $35/mo; $349/yr; 3 accounts per network; people not stated; 5,000 published videos a month; approvals not stated; trial 14 days or 10 published videos, no card.
- Pro: $79/mo; $790/yr; 10 accounts per network; people not stated; unlimited videos; approvals not stated; same trial.
- Agency: $179/mo; $1,790/yr; 25 accounts per network.
- Team column uses Pro: no plan states team members or approvals; Pro is the next plan up.

## Do NOT say
- Repurpose.io lets you upload from your device; can't send to TikTok drafts; has no failure emails.
- Repurpose.io has teams, roles, post approvals, per-brand access, MCP or AI campaigns.
- One workflow posts to several platforms.
- Prices outside pricing.ts; links; Profaily gaps.
