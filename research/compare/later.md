#### Later facts (later.com and help.later.com, checked November 25, 2026)
- Later Social posts to Instagram, Facebook, Pinterest, TikTok, LinkedIn, Threads, YouTube Shorts and Snapchat, planned on "one visual calendar"; "Drag, drop, and rearrange posts". (later.com/social-media-scheduler/, no date shown)
- One post to several profiles: pick profiles, "enter your caption, then click Customize X Posts". Works on web, iOS and Android. "Posts can't be bulk-edited." Profiles must be in the same Access Group; profiles can't be added once scheduled. (help.later.com/hc/en-us/articles/360043243873, no date shown)
- Quick Schedule: recurring weekly slots, one post per slot, on web and mobile apps; "Quick Schedule isn't available for multi-profile scheduling." Available on all plans. (help.later.com/hc/en-us/articles/360043243793; plan guide 40920284785687, no dates shown)
- Drafts count toward the monthly post count. Post tags can be added in the Post Builder (Feb 4, 2026); post tagging on Starter (20 tags), Growth (50) and Scale (unlimited) (Jan 12, 2026). Media Library labels also exist. (help.later.com/hc/en-us/p/product-updates; articles/360042771474; plan guide 40920284785687)
- Media: "Bulk upload all your photos, videos" to the Media Library; upload from "your computer, Dropbox, Google Drive or import from Canva". No CSV import found. Dropbox on all plans. (later.com/social-media-publishing/; plan guide 40920284785687)
- Video editing: "Crop, trim, & add filters to videos and photos". No compress or convert step described; upload shows "Frame rate too low" for videos under 23 FPS, which the user must fix. (later.com/social-media-publishing/; help.later.com/hc/en-us/articles/9946222186391, no date shown)
- TikTok image posts got clearer error messages, automatic resizing to TikTok requirements and a preview before scheduling (images only, not video). (help.later.com/hc/en-us/p/product-updates, Jul 2, 2026)
- Auto Publish vs notification: Instagram needs a business or creator profile; TikTok Auto Publish is enabled on desktop and needs at least one public TikTok video. Notification posts are finished in the Instagram or TikTok app. (help.later.com/hc/en-us/articles/360042772934; articles/5023195813143, no dates shown)
- Instagram Reels: on all plans including Free; Auto Publish takes one video only; Android Auto Publish capped at 90 seconds (desktop and iOS 3 minutes); auto published Reels use original audio only, per the Reels article. Audio from Meta's library can now be added while scheduling (Jul 30, 2026) and on iOS (Sep 11, 2026). (help.later.com/hc/en-us/articles/5023195813143; product-updates)
- YouTube: "Only YouTube Shorts can be scheduled"; "currently available on Later for desktop and iOS (not on Android)". Always Auto Published. Shorts 15 seconds to 3 minutes. Title (desktop steps), description, tags, category; no custom thumbnails. Free plan: 1 Short a month. (help.later.com/hc/en-us/articles/14720878941463, no date shown)
- Open item resolved: no Later page says Shorts can be scheduled from Android mobile web. Android mobile browsers are covered by browser requirements, but the Shorts article names desktop and iOS only. Keep the Android step. (help.later.com/hc/en-us/articles/360060680453, updated November 6, 2024; articles/14720878941463)
- Facebook: Business Pages only; single image, text and video on all plans; Reels and multi-photo need Starter or higher. (plan guide 40920284785687; Facebook Reels article 11605658940183 could not be loaded on this check)
- Carousels: "Multi-Photo (carousel) and Instagram Stories publishing are only available on current paid plans." (help.later.com/hc/en-us/articles/360060842914, no date shown)
- Failed posts article lists expired profile, unfinished notification post, iOS photo access, Instagram 50 Auto Publish posts in 24 hours, TikTok spam risk. No failure email, no retry button; fixes are manual. Missed notification posts can be published with "Publish Now" in the app. Notification Center is Scale only. (help.later.com/hc/en-us/articles/360060833574; articles/30384265888151; 40954654500375, no dates shown)
- Teams: "current Growth plans and higher". Roles: Account Owner, Reviewer, Member, Member (Restricted). "Approval status doesn't block publishing" unless Publish Without Approval is off for a user. External review links expire after 7 days. Access Groups: 1 on Free and Starter, unlimited on Growth and up. (help.later.com/hc/en-us/articles/40954654500375; plan guide 40920284785687, no dates shown)
- AI: Caption Writer and Ideas, run on AI credits (Starter 5, Growth 50, Scale 100 a month); Best Time to Post; Future Insights. No MCP server, ChatGPT app or Claude connector found on later.com, help.later.com or the product updates feed. (later.com/blog/ai-social-media-management/, June 16, 2026; later.com/pricing; product-updates)

## Prices (later.com/pricing, checked November 25, 2026)
- Current plans: Starter, Growth, Scale (Advanced and Enterprise are legacy plans started before July 1, 2025). 14-day free trial on all three.
- The page shows only yearly-billed prices: Starter $18.75, Growth $37.50, Scale $82.50 a month, billed yearly, with "3 months free | 25% off" for yearly. Monthly billing at 25% more gives Starter $25, Growth $50, Scale $110, matching pricing.ts; the monthly figure itself is not printed on the page. No change over 20%.
- Starter: 1 social set, 8 profiles (1 of each platform), 1 user, 30 posts per profile a month, no approvals.
- Growth: 2 social sets, 16 profiles (2 of each), 2 users, 180 posts per profile a month, "Internal & External collaboration and approvals".
- Scale: 6 social sets, 48 profiles (6 of each), 4 users, unlimited posts, unlimited Access Groups.
- Add-ons on Growth and Scale: extra social set $11.25 a month, extra user $3.75 a month (the FAQ says $5 per extra user; conflicting).
- Black Friday offer (code LATERBF26, annual Growth or Scale, new customers, ends Dec 4, 2026). Not for the page.
