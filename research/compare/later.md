#### Later facts (help.later.com, checked November 19-20, 2026)
Carried over from the pre-agent research notes. Re-verify each line on the next audit of this page.
- Schedule one post to multiple profiles; "Customize X Posts" per post; once scheduled, edit individually; no bulk edit. (help.later.com/hc/en-us/articles/360043243873)
- Auto Publish vs notification publishing; Instagram and TikTok need extra steps to enable. (360042772934, 360043360553)
- TikTok auto publish: original audio only; trending audio goes to a notification. Cover frame selection not on Android. (360053594793)
- Instagram: approved Instagram audio can Auto Publish on desktop and iOS; non-licensed audio goes to a notification.
- Reels: Auto Publish supports one video only; Android Auto Publish limited to 90 seconds. (5023195813143)
- YouTube: Shorts only; desktop and iOS, not Android. Title, description, tags, category. (14720878941463)
- Facebook Reels to Pages on paid plans. (11605658940183, checked 2026-11-20)
- Failed posts article: expired profile, notification mode, iOS camera permission, IG 50 posts a day, TikTok spam risk. No retry button or email mentioned; no pre-schedule video checks described. (360060833574)
- Teams: Growth plan and up. Roles Owner, Reviewer, Member, Member (Restricted). Approval doesn't block publishing unless Publish Without Approval is off for a user. Access Groups. (40954654500375)
- Trim video when scheduling. (later.com feature table)
