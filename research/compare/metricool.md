#### Metricool facts (metricool.com, checked November 25, 2026)

Planning and scheduling
- The calendar has Day, Week and Month views. Statuses: Draft (gray), Pending, Published/Sent, With errors (red), From autolist, plus approval statuses. (help.metricool.com/how-to-manage-your-calendar-in-metricool-xhlbw, updated 2026-11-20)
- Drafts: "The post is saved as a draft and has not been scheduled yet." (same page)
- Posts Library holds draft posts and ideas with no publishing date, up to 250 per brand, on paid plans. It has a Kanban board with status columns. (help.metricool.com/en/article/posts-library-gq1m4u/, updated 2026-10-20; metricool.com/product-updates-august-2026/)
- Tags classify posts "by theme, campaign, or format" and filter the planner. The tag system is on Starter and above. (help.metricool.com/content-planning-full-guide-faqs-qc5e1, updated 2026-11-18; help.metricool.com/tags-za9vl; metricool.com/pricing/)
- Autolists are weekly time slots. "The posts will be published in the set order." They can repeat, up to 200 posts each, and are on every plan. (help.metricool.com/schedule-content-from-an-autolist-fxehk, updated 2026-09-30; metricool.com/pricing/)
- Best times are suggested for Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, X, LinkedIn and YouTube, on every plan. (help.metricool.com/content-planning-full-guide-faqs-qc5e1; metricool.com/pricing/)
- Recurring scheduling (Advanced and Custom) generates repeated posts from a scheduling prompt. You review them, then save as draft or schedule. It can't change post content. (help.metricool.com/how-to-schedule-recurring-posts-in-metricool-r63b1, updated 2026-07-22)
- CSV bulk import works from the calendar or Autolists. Media must be a public direct link (Dropbox `?dl=0`, Google Drive `view?usp=sharing`). There is one shared Text column, and it recommends 50 posts per file. (help.metricool.com/how-to-schedule-posts-in-batch-with-a-csv-file-in-metricool-3zwxj, updated 2026-11-12)
- Multiposting "Edit per network" gives a Template tab and a tab per network. Editing a network breaks it from the template, and "use template again" restores it. (help.metricool.com/multiposting-edit-per-social-network-85out, updated 2026-06-25)

Channels and formats
- Instagram: "All videos publish as Reels." Reels run up to 15 min at 9:16. Carousels take up to 10 images and videos, and formats can be mixed. Stories are supported. (help.metricool.com/instagram-posting-errors-troubleshooting-fkhpc, updated 2026-11-05; help.metricool.com/scheduling-and-posting-options-by-social-network-cwmb3.md)
- TikTok: auto-publish needs the AUTO PUBLISH toggle. Videos run 3 s to 10 min. Photo carousels take up to 35 images. (help.metricool.com/en/article/schedule-and-post-on-tiktok-pl1qpr/, updated 2026-11-04)
- TikTok: these need manual publishing: "Effects, filters, or stickers native to TikTok" and "Music outside the Top 100 trending songs". "Sent means auto-publishing was off". (help.metricool.com/tiktok-publishing-and-upload-troubleshooting-guide-3ywdt, updated 2026-11-04)
- TikTok: "If just one word or hashtag doesn't pass the filter, TikTok discards the entire caption." The video still publishes, without text. (help.metricool.com/why-is-my-tiktok-post-published-without-text-j0qt1, updated 2026-05-14)
- YouTube: there is a title field ("Max 100 characters"). Shorts run up to 3 min, and the page warns to stay under 2:59. Long videos are supported. Images are not supported on YouTube. (help.metricool.com/schedule-and-publish-on-youtube-gof0k, updated 2026-11-17; scheduling-and-posting-options-by-social-network-cwmb3.md)
- YouTube (Sept 2026 update): Shorts thumbnails, for verified channels, and a subscriber-notification toggle. (metricool.com/product-updates-september-2026/)
- Facebook Pages: "Publish Posts, Reels (3 to 90 seconds), and Stories (up to 60 seconds)." Videos are up to 500 MB, with a title on videos and Reels. (help.metricool.com/schedule-and-publish-on-facebook-ycu1b, updated 2026-10-05)

Checks, fixes, status, failures
- Metricool checks format, size and aspect ratio when you save. If a file fails, "you'll see a red warning and won't be able to save the post." Codecs and bitrate "are validated directly by the social network when publishing." (help.metricool.com/publishing-requirements-for-images-and-videos-from-metricool-vfc8n, updated 2026-11-05)
- "HEVC files are not compatible with automatic publishing." Instagram feed and Reels videos are capped at 500 MB. TikTok takes 23 to 60 fps. (same page)
- Automatic conversion: MOV is converted to MP4, and unsupported image formats become JPG on save. No automatic video compression is mentioned. For codec or bitrate, it recommends an external editor. (same page; help.metricool.com/the-video-fails-to-publish-what-to-check-6h38v, updated 2026-05-14)
- The in-planner editor can resize, crop, trim and split video. It does not mention compression, format change or frame rate. (help.metricool.com/edit-images-and-videos-while-scheduling-your-content-wputz, updated 2026-11-17)
- Failed posts show "With errors" in red, with an error message in the post details. (calendar page above)
- The "Erroneous post" notification ("A post could not be published") goes by email and in-app, and can be configured. There is also a "Post published with warnings" notification. (help.metricool.com/notifications-in-metricool-complete-guide-ynqf8, updated 2026-10-27; new notification system: metricool.com/product-updates-september-2026/)
- No retry button is documented anywhere. The workaround is to duplicate the post and "uncheck those networks before saving the duplicate to avoid posting them twice." (help.metricool.com/why-does-my-post-appear-as-sent-in-metricool-and-not-auto-published-467xf, updated 2026-07-07). TikTok: for spam-risk errors, "Do not retry publishing from Metricool". (tiktok troubleshooting, above)

Team
- "Adding people and assigning roles is available on Advanced and Custom plans." The default roles are Super Admin, Account Manager, Analyst, Editor, Content Creator and Client, plus custom roles. (help.metricool.com/roles-and-permissions-in-metricool-rhljq, updated 2026-11-03)
- Collaborators: "You choose the brands and a role for each one." Team members hold a role across all brands. (help.metricool.com/en/article/user-management-in-metricool-xh3ius/, updated 2026-10-28)
- Approvals are on "the Advanced and Custom plans". External reviewers can be added "even if they do not have a Metricool account". Content Creators "must send all posts through the approval system". (help.metricool.com/how-to-send-posts-for-review-with-metricools-approval-system-yvdr0, updated 2026-09-22)

AI
- Metricool MCP works with Claude and ChatGPT, on any plan including Free, within plan limits. It can schedule for a set time or the best time. (help.metricool.com/faqs-about-the-metricool-mcp-1i3w0, updated 2026-10-22; help.metricool.com/more-scheduling-options-with-metricools-mcp-ylkdd, updated 2026-10-14)
- With Claude you can "draft a full week" and build a content plan for a set period. Carousel Agent: "nothing goes live without your say-so." (metricool.com/how-to-use-metricool-mcp-with-claude/, modified 2026-10-26)
- An in-app AI assistant comes with monthly credits per brand on every plan. (metricool.com/pricing/)

## Do NOT say
- That Metricool has no queue or slots. Autolists are weekly time slots, filled in order, on every plan.
- That Metricool has no drafts or labels. It has calendar drafts, a Posts Library and tags (tags on Starter and up).
- That Metricool has no carousels. It has Instagram carousels (10 items, mixed) and TikTok photo carousels (35).
- That Metricool doesn't email you on failure. The "Erroneous post" notification is email plus in-app.
- That Metricool doesn't check files before scheduling, or that you must "check requirements in the help center yourself". It checks format, size and ratio on save and blocks the save. Only codec and bitrate are left to the network.
- That a Reel "fails on file size". Size is checked on save (500 MB cap), so the documented failures are bitrate, HDR and HEVC.
- That Metricool compresses or fixes codec, bitrate or frame rate. It converts MOV to MP4 and images to JPG, and resizes and trims in its editor. Nothing more is documented.
- That Metricool has a retry button, or that it never lets you republish. The documented path is duplicate and untick.
- That TikToks with effects "automatically" go out as Sent. Those features need manual publishing, which shows as Sent.
- That MCP is paid-only. It works on every plan, Free included, within limits.
- That approvals are internal only. External reviewers need no Metricool account.
- That roles are "On the Advanced plan" only. It's Advanced and Custom (Custom includes Advanced).
- That Metricool has caption variables, or that it lacks them. Nothing is documented either way.
- That Metricool can't post long YouTube videos, or that Shorts run past 3 minutes.
- Any Metricool price.
- That Metricool added retry, pre-publish codec checks, compression or caption variables in November 2026. The November update (2026-11-24) has none of these.
