## Workflow (With Publer), sourced
- Click Create, select the TikTok, Instagram and YouTube accounts, and upload the video (from your computer or Google Drive). (/help/en/article/how-to-customize-posts-for-each-social-network-1cgw0wg/; /help/en/article/how-to-import-media-from-google-drive-1shemer/)
- Choose Reel for Instagram and Short for YouTube, and add the YouTube title in the field at the top. (/help/en/article/how-to-schedule-instagram-reels-1a7l0mu/; /help/en/article/how-to-create-public-private-or-unlisted-youtube-videosshorts-18wm3ms/)
- Use Customize Post to give each account its own text. (/help/en/article/how-to-customize-posts-for-each-social-network-1cgw0wg/)
- Set TikTok's privacy and Comment/Duet/Stitch for this video; TikTok's API allows no defaults. (/help/en/article/managing-tiktok-accounts-in-publer-1gloujj)
- Schedule it or AutoSchedule it. If one account fails, it's marked Failed with the reason: Reuse the post, fix it and schedule it again. (/help/en/article/how-to-schedule-posts-15atdj7; /help/en/article/how-to-find-my-failed-posts-1fp2zfb/)

## What Publer is good at
- Breadth of networks and post types (Reels, Shorts, Stories, carousels, Trial Reels, Facebook Pages), from one composer, on web and mobile.
- Strong scheduling toolkit: posting schedules with labeled slots, AutoSchedule, Recycle, Recurring, and bulk scheduling of up to 500 posts by CSV or bulk upload.
- Team workflows: Owner/Admin/Editor/Client roles, per-account access, and Admin or Client approval.
- AI everywhere: AI Assist with Brand Voice, a built-in photo and video editor, and an MCP server for Claude and ChatGPT.
- Fair hero line: "Publer is a broad scheduler for every network, with bulk tools, a media library, client approvals and AI Assist."

## Do NOT say
- That Publer compresses videos or converts frame rate: its pages say it only re-encodes for API compatibility and elsewhere that it "does not alter your media files in any way."
- That Publer checks every platform's requirements before scheduling: only TikTok account limits and Reel-type detection are documented.
- That Publer has caption variables: signatures are static text per account.
- That Publer turns images into a YouTube video: not shown (VistaCreate templates for making videos are mentioned, not image-to-video).
- That retry is one tap or targets only the failed platform: the docs describe Reuse, edit and reschedule.
- That you must upload the video separately per platform, or that YouTube needs a separate form: the docs show one composer.
- Plan gating for approvals, roles, bulk/CSV or labels: /plans doesn't map them clearly to a plan.
- Any TikTok size limit: two help articles disagree (1 GB vs 2 GB).
- That the MCP handles approvals or bulk actions: not mentioned.
- That Publer adds TikTok sounds or Instagram trending audio in direct posting: its docs say it can't.
