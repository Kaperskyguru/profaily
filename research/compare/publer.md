## What Publer is good at
- Breadth of networks (13) and post types (Reels, Shorts, Stories, carousels, Trial Reels, Facebook Pages), from one composer, on web and mobile, with app reminders for what the APIs can't do.
- Strong scheduling toolkit: posting schedules with labeled slots, AutoSchedule, Recycle, Recurring, and bulk scheduling of up to 500 posts by CSV or bulk upload.
- Team workflows: Owner/Admin/Editor/Client roles, per-account access, Admin or Client approval, approvals on mobile.
- Failed posts handled per account: the others stay published and the failed one can be rescheduled on its own.
- AI everywhere: AI Assist with Brand Voice, AI-content labels, a built-in photo and video editor, and an MCP server for Claude and ChatGPT.
- Fair hero line: "Publer is a broad scheduler for over a dozen networks, with bulk tools, client approvals and AI built in."

## Do NOT say
- That Publer compresses videos or converts frame rate: its pages say it only re-encodes for API compatibility and elsewhere that it "does not alter your media files in any way."
- That Publer checks every platform's requirements before scheduling: only TikTok account limits, Reel-type detection and composer issue warnings are documented.
- That Publer has full caption variables: its shortcodes are per account and its mention shortcodes resolve per network, inserted by hand.
- That Publer turns images into a YouTube video: not shown.
- That Publer can't retry just the failed platform: since its 28-29/09/2026 articles, a failed account's post can be rescheduled on its own while the others stay published. It has no "Retry" button.
- That you must upload the video separately per platform, or that YouTube needs a separate form: the docs show one composer.
- That the MCP handles approvals or bulk actions: not mentioned.
- That Publer adds TikTok sounds or Instagram trending audio in direct posting: its docs say it can't (app reminders can).
- That Publer has no free trial: Professional has 7 days, Business 14 days (help center, 15/09/2026).
- Any TikTok size limit: help articles have disagreed (1 GB vs 2 GB).
- A Publer price other than what pricing.ts holds: the live billing API wasn't read.
