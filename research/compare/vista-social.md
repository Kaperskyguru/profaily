#### Vista Social facts (vistasocial.com, checked November 21, 2026)
Help articles are on support.vistasocial.com and show no updated date; /insights posts show publish dates.
- Live and shipping: "Introducing Ads Manager in Ask Vista" (Nov 16, 2026); "We Shipped 100+ Major Features in 2026" (Sep 25, 2026). (/insights/new-feature/; /insights/vista-social-2026-year-in-review/)
- Post types: TikTok single image, carousel, video, multi-video; Instagram video/Reel, carousel, Stories; YouTube video only; Facebook Pages video/Reel. "Support varies based on each network's API limitations." (/hc/en-us/articles/49453905957915)
- TikTok: auto-publish ("Uploaded. Pending publication", then Published) or a reminder notification to post from your phone. Settings: up to 5 comments, comments/duets/stitches toggles, Promotional content or Paid partnership labels. (/hc/en-us/articles/4419165964827)
- TikTok trending audio and location tags when scheduling (Aug 2026). (/insights/vista-social-2026-year-in-review/)
- TikTok carousels up to 35 images; queues with predefined times. (/integrations/tiktok)
- Instagram: auto-publish or reminder; "By default, a video will be published as a Reel"; carousels up to 20 images/videos. (/hc/en-us/articles/4430358197275; /hc/en-us/articles/7117217001115)
- YouTube: "Video title", privacy, category, playlist, tags, first comment, made for kids; preview as classic video or Short; Shorts "under 60 seconds"; single video posts only. (/hc/en-us/articles/4475062292763)
- Facebook Pages: Reels and videos. (/hc/en-us/articles/4430269950619; /hc/en-us/articles/49453905957915)
- Calendar: "monthly, daily, or weekly view", filters by status, users, labels, content and queue. (/insights/getting-started-with-vista-social-calendar-overview/, Dec 14, 2022)
- Calendar display options plus List and Table views filtered by Drafts, Scheduled, Published or Failed. (/hc/en-us/articles/4495695550363)
- Composer: Publish now, a date and time (one for all or per profile), Queue, Optimal Time, Save as draft, Send for approval. (/insights/getting-started-with-vista-social-scheduling/, Feb 22, 2024)
- Queues: time slots per profile for any day; "Queue next", "Queue last", "'Queue available' will find the next open slot in your queue". Queue labels exist. (/hc/en-us/articles/4411057807643; /hc/en-us/articles/34069599020315)
- Draft posts, post labeling and queue labels on every plan; bulk labeling. (/pricing; /hc/en-us/articles/16771583636379)
- Bulk publishing: CSV import, "Import media" (several images or videos become separate posts), RSS and more, "Generate with AI". Import media from computer, library, Google Drive, Dropbox or OneDrive. (/hc/en-us/articles/4411200242203)
- CSV `link` column takes a public image or video URL. (/hc/en-us/articles/10295276622875)
- Per-network captions: pick a network in the preview pane and edit its caption; deleting custom text reverts it. (/hc/en-us/articles/13931658357531)
- Custom fields: "dynamic fields like URLs, locations, and websites" "unique to each of your social profiles", with defaults, inserted from the composer. (/hc/en-us/articles/14439524021531)
- Video processing on by default for Twitter, Instagram and Facebook: "auto adjusting the video to fit the necessary specs", "may result in a slight reduction in video quality"; can be turned off. (/hc/en-us/articles/10208587806363)
- Instagram processing converts to MP4/H.264/AAC, adjusts frame rate and bitrate, trims over-length video, scales down, pads ratio. (/hc/en-us/articles/7835518092187)
- Failed TikTok posts: "The error TikTok sent back shows on the post." Frame-rate errors: re-export at 23-60 FPS and re-upload. (/hc/en-us/articles/39618661768987)
- Failed posts: filter Calendar by Failed or use Dashboard Alerts, then "Duplicate post" to schedule or publish again. (/hc/en-us/articles/14548254932891)
- Notification Center: "Post failure notification – When a post fails to publish". Browser notifications for failed posts. (/hc/en-us/articles/36197454821403; /hc/en-us/articles/5801097675035)
- Email notifications: per-type tick boxes, all enabled by default; no page names a failed-post email type. (/hc/en-us/articles/28101273363227; /hc/en-us/articles/17200645206683)
- Roles: Admin, Profile Group admin, Restricted user; Contributor permission "able to schedule posts but will require review". (/hc/en-us/articles/4409604728219)
- Profile groups "are like containers for a brand, client, group, or project"; users can be limited to profile groups. (/hc/en-us/articles/11328133941915)
- Approvals: Contributors "cannot publish posts directly"; single-step or multi-step workflows; "Professional (Single-step)". (/hc/en-us/articles/46431598676251)
- MCP for Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor and others; schedules posts; "checks character limits, missing media, and formatting issues before anything publishes"; on all paid plans. (/hc/en-us/articles/40393513382043; /integrations/mcp/)
- Campaigns: Ask Vista drafts per-platform variants from a brief, makes visuals and schedules; "Nothing publishes without your approval." (/insights/plan-social-media-campaign-fast/, Jul 21, 2026)
- Suite: unified inbox, reviews, listening, reports, link in bio, white label. (/pricing)

## Prices (vistasocial.com/pricing, checked November 21, 2026)
- USD ("$"), no currency or region switching on the page. Flat bundles of profiles, not per profile. "Save 20% with yearly billing". 14-day trial, no card.
- Professional: $99/mo, $80/mo billed yearly; brands not stated; 15 social profiles; 2 users; unlimited posts; single-step approval; 14-day trial.
- Advanced: $199/mo, $160/mo billed yearly; 30 profiles; 3 users; unlimited posts; multi-stage approvals; 14-day trial.
- Scale: $449/mo, $360/mo billed yearly; 70 profiles; 4 users; multi-stage approvals; 14-day trial.
- Enterprise: Custom; unlimited profiles; 8+ users; demo.
- Price table: Professional is both the cheapest paid plan and the cheapest with team members and approvals, so it fills both columns.

## Do NOT say
- "Starts at $64/month" (stale meta text) or any Enterprise price.
- Pricing is per profile; there is a free plan.
- It compresses or converts video for TikTok or YouTube (processing documented for Twitter, Instagram, Facebook only).
- Its video fix is one tap (it is automatic).
- The composer checks each platform before scheduling (only the MCP check is documented).
- It emails you when a post fails (not named on its pages).
- Retry targets only the failed platform.
- It turns images into a YouTube video.
- Drag-and-drop rescheduling; a TikTok privacy setting; a single CSV limit; AI credit numbers.
- The MCP plans campaigns (Ask Vista does, in the app).
