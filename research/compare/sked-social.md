#### Sked Social facts (skedsocial.com, checked November 26, 2026)
Help articles on help.skedsocial.com show "Updated" dates. Feature, integration and pricing pages show no date (pricing says competitor prices were "checked June and July 2026"; footer "© 2026"). Sked Social is a current product ("Built in Melbourne, shipping to the world since 2014"; /pricing) that auto-publishes to TikTok, Instagram and YouTube.

Networks and publishing
- Auto-publishing to Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, LinkedIn, YouTube, Pinterest, X, Google Business Profile and Threads; notification-based publishing for Snapchat. "9 supported channels." (/pricing FAQ and plan table)
- Snapchat and Threads posts are sent as reminders to publish manually "because automatic publishing isn't supported for them yet"; reminders go by email and/or push to the Sked app. (help.skedsocial.com/failed-or-successfully-published-posts, "How post notifications work in Sked", updated June 11, 2025). Note: this help page conflicts with /pricing, which lists Threads under auto-publishing.
- TikTok: "Native auto-publish for both brand and creator accounts", "No phone reminder". Privacy, comment and duet controls set pre-publish; TikTok Photo Mode carousels publish natively. (/integrations/tiktok-scheduler)
- TikTok specs: .mp4/.mov, max 1GB, 3 to 600 seconds, 23 to 60 FPS, recommended 1080x1920; carousels up to 10 images, images only. Audio must be in the file: "external sound URLs are not supported." TikTok caps 20 posts per account per rolling 24 hours. (help.skedsocial.com/tiktok-auto-publishing-specifications-sked-social-help-center, updated January 7, 2025)
- Adding a TikTok account: + add Account, choose login, then "schedule content for automatic posting." (help.skedsocial.com/migrate-your-tiktok-account-from-notification-channels-to-auto-publishing-sked-social-help-center, updated December 5, 2025)
- Instagram: "Feed posts, carousels, Reels and Stories publish at the time you set"; Reels 5 to 90 seconds, 9:16, .MOV or .MP4; custom Reel cover; carousels up to 20 slides. (/integrations/instagram-scheduler)
- Trial Reels have their own help article. (help.skedsocial.com/view-schedule-posts-calendar-queue-planner, section index)
- YouTube: "Pick the date and time and save; the video publishes automatically." "One pipeline for both YouTube formats" (Shorts and long-form). Title and description added when creating the post; custom thumbnails on verified channels. (/integrations/youtube)
- YouTube post form: video title, category, description and tags; "video will auto-post on your scheduled time"; only the channel owner can connect the channel. (help.skedsocial.com/adding-your-youtube-channel-sked-social-help-center, updated October 28, 2026)
- Facebook: Pages; "Text, images, videos, Reels, carousels and link posts all auto-publish to Facebook from the same Create post screen"; Stories too. (/integrations/facebook-scheduler)

Scheduling, calendar, drafts, labels, import
- Calendar: Month, Week and Day views, past and upcoming posts; drag posts to change their time; drafts sit on the left and drag onto a date to schedule; Instagram Grid preview. (help.skedsocial.com/calendar, updated October 28, 2026)
- Rebuilt calendar filters by channel, label or account, with an agenda pane beside month view. (/blog/new-the-sked-social-calendar-rebuilt)
- Queue: a weekly repeating schedule of time slots per account; "Queue post" sends a post to the next available slot; can mix with posts at set times; best-time suggestions. (help.skedsocial.com/using-the-queue-sked-social-help-center, updated October 28, 2026)
- Automatic posting queue, bulk scheduling and visual planner are on every plan. (/pricing)
- Labels (content pillars and campaigns) work across accounts, several per post, filter the calendar and library; included on every paid plan. (/features/labels)
- Bulk create lives in Create Post: add several images or videos, choose bulk (one post per file) or single (one post with many media); each post opens in its own tab to finish as draft, schedule or queue. CSV not mentioned. (help.skedsocial.com/new-and-improved-bulk-create, updated September 10, 2025)
- Google Drive: selected folders sync into the media library hourly, read-only; "Workflow integrations are rolling out to pilot accounts first." (/integrations/google-drive)
- Dropbox: folder sync into a Library collection every 60 minutes, at original resolution; requires Accelerate or higher. Video not explicitly mentioned. (help.skedsocial.com/help-doc-how-to-connect-dropbox-and-sync-a-folder-to-your-sked-library, dated September 1, 2026)
- No page found describing CSV import of posts.
- Round 3 reviewer (26 Nov 2026): the Drive and Dropbox help docs (Sept 1, 2026) say "A Sked account on the Accelerate plan or above" and promise "Images and media files"; the Drive "pilot" line on /integrations/google-drive is older. Google Sheets rows can come in as ideas (/integrations/google-sheets-social-media-integration).
- MCP agent seats are paid per seat; Grow is limited to 3 seats. (help center agent-seats page, November 19, 2026)

Captions and media
- Common (linked) caption posts to every platform; toggle "Customize (platform name) post" to give a platform its own text. "Each unlinked platform gets its own draft or post, and these are scheduled separately on the calendar." (help.skedsocial.com/how-does-common-caption-work-sked-social-help-center, updated December 2, 2025)
- "Write once, customise per channel, schedule to every channel." Built-in editor to "Trim, crop and edit clips." The editor describes no compression or format conversion; separately, uploads are transcoded and downscaled (see Before and after posting). (/features/scheduling)
- No page found for caption variables/placeholders or turning images into a YouTube video.

Before and after posting
- Instagram image limits: "You'll see a notification in Sked if your image exceeds these limits", with a button to crop to the right ratio. Images only; when the notice appears isn't stated. (help.skedsocial.com/portrait-images-or-videos, October 22, 2026)
- Video editor: "You can upload your own additional videos, images and audio" and join them on a timeline into one video. (help.skedsocial.com/video-editing-on-sked-how-to-common-actions-and-faq, December 10, 2025)
- MCP: example "Drafting three posts for next week's most active times"; commit_create_draft and schedule_draft are Full tools. (/features/mcp)
- Archives show each post's status and "any errors returned" by the platform; for a failed post, "use 'post again' on the failed post". (help.skedsocial.com/how-to-fix-your-facebook-posts-not-getting-published-reconnect-pages-sked-social-help-center; found by reviewer 26 Nov 2026)
- A post to several platforms "will branch out into their own separate scheduled posts", so status and post again are per platform. (help.skedsocial.com/posting-to-multiple-platforms-at-the-same-time-sked-social-help-center)
- Uploaded video is transcoded: "We transcode (reformat) the video after you upload it"; everything is downscaled to a maximum width of 1080 pixels. Frame-rate or container conversion for TikTok not described. (help.skedsocial.com/how-long-or-short-can-your-videos-be)
- Approvals add-ons can be added to Grow (no price stated) (help.skedsocial.com/difference-approvals-addons, May 4, 2026); "New Grow and Accelerate plan customers have the approval workflow enabled" (help.skedsocial.com/approvals). /pricing lists only post statuses and previews on Grow.
- Failed posts: Sked emails when it can't publish (e.g. expired token, network outage). Failure emails always go to the administrator, plus up to 5 more addresses per account. (help.skedsocial.com/failed-or-successfully-published-posts, updated June 11, 2025; help.skedsocial.com/setting-up-successful-post-email-notifications-or-sending-failure-notifications-to-others-sked-social-help-center, updated December 2, 2025)
- "Potential error": a post Sked sent but can't confirm went live; shown with a warning symbol in archives and emailed. Fix: check on the platform ("the platform usually explains why"), then post manually or click "post again" in archives to reschedule. Sked retries automatically only when it knows the cause (e.g. dropped connection). (help.skedsocial.com/what-is-a-potential-error-for-an-instagram-post-sked-social-help-center, updated August 17, 2026)

Team and approvals
- Users: Grow 3, Accelerate 6 (more at extra cost), Custom custom. Access scoping per profile on Grow; per profile plus role, with feature-level control by role, on Accelerate and Custom. (/pricing)
- Permissions are individual toggles (inbox, reports, adding accounts, library collections, external review links, post status restrictions, listening); only "Administrator" is named. Post Status Restrictions can hide the approved status from users without approval rights. (help.skedsocial.com/user-account-permissions-sked-social-help-center, updated January 6, 2026)
- Approvals: custom statuses; content approved "will automatically go live at the time you've selected"; clients approve or request changes from a no-login review link; read-only shared calendars. (help.skedsocial.com/approvals, updated March 31, 2026)
- Internal and external approvals on Accelerate and Custom only; Grow has 2 fixed post statuses and previews. Verified reviewers and multi-step approvals on Custom. (/pricing)

AI apps
- Sked Social MCP works with Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor, Copilot and other MCP clients. Full tools include commit_create_draft, schedule_draft, update_post (status, copy, time), review_calendar; "your approvals still apply." Core tools (Brand Kit, ideas) come with every plan; full tools "need the Sked Social MCP full-access add-on and an agent seat for each person who connects" (price not stated). Custom includes 1 full agent seat; Grow lists "Limited MCP access." (/features/mcp; /pricing)
- Planner groups ideas into a campaign across content pillars; Smart assist drafts captions, briefs and concepts. No page describes AI planning and scheduling a whole campaign. (/features/planner)

## Prices (skedsocial.com/pricing, checked November 26, 2026)

Unlimited scheduling. Internal and external approvals, no-login review portal. TikTok + Instagram + YouTube: US$249/mo.

## Workflow (With Sked), sourced
- Open Create Post, add the TikTok, Instagram and YouTube accounts, and add the video from your computer or the Library (where synced Drive or Dropbox folders land). (help.skedsocial.com/new-and-improved-bulk-create; /integrations/google-drive)
- Write one common caption; to change it for a platform, click it and toggle "Customize (platform) post". Each customized platform becomes its own post, scheduled separately on the calendar. (help.skedsocial.com/how-does-common-caption-work-sked-social-help-center)
- Fill in YouTube's title, category, description and tags. (help.skedsocial.com/adding-your-youtube-channel-sked-social-help-center)
- Set TikTok's privacy, comment and duet controls; audio must already be in the video file. (/integrations/tiktok-scheduler; help.skedsocial.com/tiktok-auto-publishing-specifications-sked-social-help-center)
- Pick a time, or Queue post to take the next slot. If a post fails, Sked emails the admin; open archives to see the error and use "post again" on the failed post. (help.skedsocial.com/using-the-queue-sked-social-help-center; help.skedsocial.com/failed-or-successfully-published-posts; help.skedsocial.com/what-is-a-potential-error-for-an-instagram-post-sked-social-help-center)

## What Sked Social is good at
- Agency and multi-brand approvals: custom post statuses, no-login client review links, read-only shared calendars, multi-step approvals on Custom.
- Instagram-first planning: Grid preview, Stories, product tags, first comments, Trial Reels, 20-slide carousels.
- Broad auto-publishing across 9 networks, including TikTok carousels and YouTube Shorts and long videos, plus inbox, social listening and reporting in one tool.
- Ideas board with content pillars and campaign labels, and an MCP server for AI agents.
- Fair hero line: "Sked Social is an all-in-one social workspace for agencies and multi-location brands, with client approvals, inbox, listening and reporting alongside auto-publishing to nine networks."

## Do NOT say
- That Sked has no team plan or that Grow is single-user: Grow includes 3 users.
- That Sked's cheapest price is US$58 a month on monthly billing: that is the yearly-billing figure; monthly is US$69.
- That Sked needs phone reminders for TikTok, Instagram Reels or YouTube: all three auto-publish.
- That YouTube is Shorts only: its page says Shorts and long-form share one pipeline.
- That Sked has no MCP: it has one; full scheduling tools need a paid add-on and agent seat.
- That Sked imports posts from a CSV: no page shows CSV import.
- That Sked adds TikTok sounds: the specs page says external sound URLs are not supported (the marketing page mentions setting "the sound").
- That approvals are on Grow: they are Accelerate and Custom only.
- That Sked converts frame rate or format for TikTok: only transcoding and downscaling to 1080 wide are described.
- That Sked has no video processing: it transcodes and downscales uploads.
- That Threads is manual or automatic without hedging: /pricing says auto-publishing, a June 2025 help article says reminders.
- That Google Drive sync is available to everyone: the page says it is rolling out to pilot accounts first.
- A price for the MCP full-access add-on: not stated.
