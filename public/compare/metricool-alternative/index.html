Choose for your workflow

When Metricool is the better fit—and when Profaily is

The best choice depends on the work your team needs to get done every week.

Choose Metricool if

Multi-brand social management with analytics, competitor analysis, reports, publishing, inbox, and team controls by plan.

You need analytics and reporting across many networks or brands, competitor tracking, or a free way to manage a small social footprint. Metricool’s official pricing currently lists a free tier for one brand and 20 scheduled posts per month; paid tiers expand brand limits, and Advanced adds team/client management, roles, and approvals.

Choose Profaily if

You want video operations connected to business follow-through.

You want each finished video to have one connected path from intake through platform-specific publishing and destination status to eligible social conversations, lead qualification, and what your team should test next.

Know the trade-off

Focus means a deliberate scope.

Metricool covers a broad set of social and advertising channels and has strong reporting breadth. Profaily is narrower in channel coverage and is not a general ads, web analytics, or white-label reporting suite; its focus is short-form video operations and follow-through.

Questions teams ask

Profaily vs Metricool: frequently asked questions

Use these answers as a starting point, then confirm any plan-specific requirements with the providers.

Is Profaily better than Metricool?

They serve different priorities. Metricool is a strong fit for analytics, reporting, competitor analysis, broad channel support, and multi-brand management. Profaily focuses on short-form video publishing and the workflow from a video to audience response and lead follow-through.

Does Metricool have a free plan?

Metricool’s official pricing page currently lists a free tier for one brand and up to 20 scheduled posts per month. Connected networks and other limits differ by plan.

Does Metricool have a unified inbox?

Metricool’s published pricing comparison lists inbox access for Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, and YouTube. Check the current plan table for channel and feature availability.

Which Profaily plan includes team workflows?

Profaily Teams is designed for shared workspaces, roles, review, lead ownership, routing, and CRM handoff. Profaily currently has no free plan and offers a five-day trial.
