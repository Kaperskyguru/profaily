Good to know

TikTok publishing, clearly explained.

Profaily respects the capabilities and permissions of each connected platform.

Can I customize the TikTok post separately?

Yes. The TikTok destination has its own caption and supported publishing controls while using the same source video.

Can Profaily publish publicly on every TikTok account?

Publishing visibility and actions depend on TikTok app access, account eligibility, permissions, and the options TikTok returns for that account.

Can TikTok comments become leads?

Eligible comments and messages can enter Profaily’s conversation and lead workflows where the platform makes the required access available.
