FAQ

Choose the right way to work.

Both plans start with a five-day free trial. There is no free plan.

Which plan is right for a solo creator?

Pro is designed for creators and independent operators who plan, publish, and manage their own video workflow.

When should a business choose Teams?

Choose Teams when video work is shared across people or brands and you need roles, review, ownership, lead routing, and CRM handoff.

Do both plans publish to all four supported platforms?

Both plans cover TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, and Facebook. Available publishing options can vary by account permissions and platform support.

Does Profaily edit or resize my video?

No. Upload your finished video. Profaily reuses that source file and lets you customize the available post details for each platform.

Is there a free plan?

No. Pro and Teams both start with a five-day free trial.
