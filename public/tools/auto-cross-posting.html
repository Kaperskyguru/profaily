FAQ

Auto Cross Post, explained.

Know what happens before you connect a channel.

Do I need to log into Profaily every time I publish?

No. Once your accounts and sharing routes are set up, you can publish new videos on the selected main platform as usual. Profaily handles eligible cross-posting to your configured destinations.

Which platforms can I use as the main account?

The planned source platforms are TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, and YouTube. The destinations available for each source depend on the connected account and supported platform routes.

Will every video be shared to every destination?

You choose the destinations for a flow. A video may be ineligible for a particular destination if that platform’s account permissions, media requirements, or format limits are not met.

Does Profaily change my video for each platform?

Auto Cross Post distributes eligible source videos to selected destinations. The precise media adaptation options depend on Profaily’s supported routes; platform requirements may differ.
