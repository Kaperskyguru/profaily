FAQ

Smart Repost, explained.

Know what happens to a video before it returns to rotation.

Where does Smart Repost get the videos?

It selects from eligible videos in your Profaily library. The criteria you define determine which videos are considered for a repost.

How is the next video selected?

Smart Repost randomly selects from the videos that match your criteria. It does not automatically favor the highest-performing video.

Does Smart Repost change my original video?

No. The original video remains in your Profaily library. Smart Repost refreshes the accompanying hook, message, and call to action.

Can I decide which videos are eligible?

Yes. Define the criteria for the videos you want included in the rotation. Review and adjust your rules as your content and campaigns change.

Where will the refreshed video be distributed?

It is redistributed through the connected channels you select. Available actions depend on platform support, account permissions, and the requirements for each post.
