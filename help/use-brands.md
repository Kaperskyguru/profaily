A **brand** is one business or client in Profaily. Each brand has its own connected accounts, posting schedule, timezone and videos, so nothing gets posted to the wrong place.

- The **Creator** plan includes **1 brand**.
- The **Business** plan includes **3 brands**.

You'll find all your brands in **Settings → Brands**, each with the accounts it posts to:

![Settings, Brands, listing Lumen Coffee with no channels yet and Profaily with TikTok, Instagram and YouTube, marked Current, and New brand](/assets/help/use-brands/brands.jpg)

## Add a brand {#add}

1. Tap the brand name at the top left to open the brand list. On a phone, open the menu (☰) first.
2. Tap **Add brand** and give it a name.
3. Connect that brand's accounts: tap the **···** next to it, then **General → Connect a channel**.

## Switch between brands {#switch}

Tap the brand name at the top left and choose another. The composer, queue and calendar then show only that brand.

![The brand list open at the top left, with a Personal brand, the team's Profaily brand, and Add brand, Create new team and Settings below](/assets/help/use-brands/brand-switcher.jpg)

## Change a brand's settings {#settings}

Tap the **···** next to a brand to open its settings:

- **General:** its name, picture and connected accounts.
- **Scheduling:** its posting times, used by **Next slot**, and its timezone.

Under **Scheduling**, **Quick setup** builds your weekly slots for you. Pick a rhythm such as **Once a day** or **Morning & evening**, or set how many times a day and between which hours, then tap **Arrange my queue**.

![A brand's Scheduling settings, with the Africa › Lagos timezone, Quick setup rhythms and the Arrange my queue button](/assets/help/use-brands/scheduling.jpg)

## Give people access to some brands only {#access}

On the Business plan, each team member only opens the brands you give them. [Choose brands when you invite](/help/invite-your-team#brands).
