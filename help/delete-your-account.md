Deleting your account removes everything at once, and it **can't be undone**.

## What gets deleted {#what}

- Every connected account is disconnected, and Profaily asks each platform to cancel its access.
- Scheduled videos are cancelled.
- Your uploaded videos are deleted from storage.
- Your account, posts and sign-in are removed.

Videos already posted stay on TikTok, Instagram and YouTube. Delete those on each platform if you want them gone.

## Delete it {#delete}

1. In Profaily, open the menu, tap your account, then **Settings**.
2. Tap **Your user**, then **Delete account**.
3. Confirm.

If you only want to stop paying, [cancel your plan](/help/change-or-cancel-your-plan) instead. Your account stays.

## Ask us to do it {#ask}

Email [privacy@useprofaily.com](mailto:privacy@useprofaily.com) from the email address on your account. We'll delete it within 7 days and confirm when it's done. The full detail is in the [Privacy Policy](https://app.useprofaily.com/privacy).

## Frequently asked questions {#faq}

### Can I get my account back after deleting it?

No. Deleting is immediate and can't be undone. If you only want to stop paying, cancel your plan instead.

### Can Profaily delete it for me?

Yes. Email privacy@useprofaily.com from the email address on your account and we'll do it within 7 days, then confirm.
