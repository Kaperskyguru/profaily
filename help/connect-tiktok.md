You need the **Admin** role in your Profaily team to connect an account. Any TikTok account can be connected.

## Connect TikTok in Profaily {#connect}

1. In Profaily, open the menu, tap your account, then **Settings**.
2. Tap **Brands**, then the **···** next to the brand you post for.
3. Tap **General**, then **Connect a channel**.
4. Next to TikTok, tap **Connect**.
5. TikTok opens. Sign in, check what Profaily is asking for, and approve it.
6. You come straight back to Profaily, and TikTok shows under **Channels**.

![The Connect a channel window in Profaily, with a Connect button next to TikTok, Instagram and YouTube](/assets/help/connect-tiktok/connect-dialog.jpg)

New to Profaily? The first time you sign in, Profaily asks you to connect an account. The steps from 4 onwards are the same.

## What Profaily can do on TikTok {#permissions}

Profaily asks TikTok for two permissions: `user.info.basic` to see your basic account details, and `video.publish` to publish your videos. It never sees your TikTok password.

TikTok has reviewed Profaily and approved it for public posting, so your videos aren't limited to private.

[Read how Profaily keeps your accounts safe](/blog/is-profaily-safe-to-connect-social-media-accounts).

## Choose who can see each video {#audience}

When you schedule a video for TikTok, you choose its audience, just like in the TikTok app. The choices come from your own TikTok account settings. If TikTok says an audience isn't available for your account, pick another one and try again.

## If it doesn't connect {#troubleshooting}

If Profaily shows one of these messages, here's what to do:

- **"TikTok didn't grant every permission Profaily needs."** Connect again and leave every permission switched on.
- **"Connection cancelled."** The TikTok window was closed before you approved the connection. Connect again.
- **"That took too long."** Connect again and finish within a few minutes.
- **"TikTok wasn't connected: your plan has room for no more channels."** Disconnect a channel you no longer use, or [change your plan](/pricing).
- **"TikTok isn't responding right now."** TikTok is having trouble. Try again in a few minutes.

## TikTok's video rules {#rules}

TikTok checks every video before it publishes:

- **Format:** MP4, encoded as H.264.
- **Frame rate:** between 23 and 60 frames per second.
- **Resolution:** between 360 and 4,096 pixels on each side.
- **Length:** what's allowed depends on your TikTok account. If a video is too long for yours, trim it and retry.
- **Daily limit:** TikTok limits how many videos an account can post each day. If you reach it, try again the next day.

TikTok also screens captions. If it flags a caption as spammy, edit the caption and retry.

## Frequently asked questions {#faq}

### Will my TikTok videos be public?

Yes, if you choose that audience. TikTok has approved Profaily for public posting, so your videos go out to whoever you pick, the same as posting in the app.

### Does Profaily get my TikTok password?

No. You sign in on TikTok's own page, and TikTok gives Profaily permission to publish videos and read basic account details.
