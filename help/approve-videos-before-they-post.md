Approvals let an Editor prepare videos while someone else decides what goes out. They're part of the **Business** plan.

## How it works {#how}

1. An **Editor** uploads a video, writes the captions, and taps **Send for review**. It's marked **In review**.
2. A **Producer** or **Admin** opens it and either:
   - taps **Approve and schedule**, which schedules it, or
   - taps **Request changes**, which sends it back to the Editor marked **Changes requested**.
3. The Editor makes the changes and sends it for review again.

An Editor can never schedule or publish a video, so nothing goes out without a Producer or Admin saying yes.

## Who can do what {#who}

- **Editors** upload, write captions and send for review.
- **Producers** schedule their own videos and approve or send back an Editor's.
- **Admins** do everything a Producer can, and manage accounts and people.

[Set up roles for your team](/help/invite-your-team).

## Frequently asked questions {#faq}

### Can an Editor post without approval?

No. An Editor can never schedule or publish. Their videos go out only after a Producer or Admin approves them.

### Which plan has approvals?

Approvals are part of the Business plan, including during the 14-day free trial.
