Teams are part of the **Business** plan, which has room for **5 people**. Only an **Admin** can invite people or change their roles.

## Invite someone {#invite}

1. In Profaily, open your account menu (your name, bottom left) and choose **Settings**. On a phone, open the menu (☰) first.
2. Tap **Team**, then **Invite**.

![Settings, Team, listing the team's people with their roles, and the Invite button](/assets/help/invite-your-team/team.jpg)

3. In **Invite people**, type one or more email addresses.
4. Choose a **Role**, and tick the **Brands they can open**.

![The Invite people window with an email address typed, Editor chosen as the role and the Profaily brand ticked](/assets/help/invite-your-team/invite-people.jpg)

5. Tap **Send invite**. You'll see "Invite sent to" and their email address.

![Settings, Team, with the message Invite sent to olivia@example.com](/assets/help/invite-your-team/invite-sent.jpg)

Here's the whole invite, recorded in the app:

![Inviting a team member: Settings, Team, Invite, type the email, keep Editor, Send invite](/assets/help/invite-your-team/invite-flow.mp4)

Each person gets an email with a link that works for **7 days**. If it runs out, send a new invite. To take one back before it's accepted, tap **Revoke** next to it.

## The four roles {#roles}

| Role | What they can do |
| --- | --- |
| **Admin** | Connect, reconnect and disconnect accounts; invite and remove people; everything a Producer can do |
| **Producer** | Schedule videos; approve an Editor's video, which schedules it, or ask for changes |
| **Editor** | Upload videos and write captions, then send them for review; can never schedule |
| **Viewer** | See the brands they're given, change nothing |

Roles are checked by Profaily's servers on every action, not just hidden in the app.

## Choose which brands each person sees {#brands}

If you run more than one brand, each person only opens the brands you give them. Choose them when you invite, and change them later from **Settings → Team**.

## When someone leaves {#leaves}

Remove them from **Settings → Team**. Their access ends straight away, and your connected accounts stay connected, because your team never had the passwords.

## Frequently asked questions {#faq}

### How many people can I invite?

The Business plan has room for 5 people, counting invites that haven't been accepted yet. The Creator plan is just you.

### Do my team members need my social media passwords?

No. Only an Admin connects accounts, and they sign in on each platform's own page. Everyone else posts through Profaily without ever seeing a password.
