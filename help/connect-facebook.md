You need the **Admin** role in your Profaily team to connect an account. On Facebook, you need a role on each Page you want to publish to. Personal profiles can't be connected: Facebook only lets apps publish to Pages.

## Connect Facebook in Profaily {#connect}

1. In Profaily, open your account menu (your name, bottom left) and choose **Settings**. On a phone, open the menu first.
2. Tap **Brands**, then the **···** next to the brand you post for.
3. Tap **General**, then **Connect a channel**.
4. Next to Facebook, tap **Connect**.
5. Facebook opens. Sign in, choose the Pages Profaily can publish to, and approve it.
6. You come straight back to Profaily, and Facebook shows under **Channels**.

## Choose the Page new videos go to {#pages}

If you shared more than one Page, Profaily connects each of them. In the brand's settings, open **New drafts**: Facebook shows how many Pages are connected, with a menu to choose the Page new videos start with.

Connected a single Page earlier and want the rest? Tap **Add Pages** next to Facebook in **New drafts**, and choose the other Pages on Facebook.

## What Profaily can do on Facebook {#permissions}

Profaily asks Facebook for permission to list your Pages (`pages_show_list`), publish to them (`pages_manage_posts`), read them back (`pages_read_engagement`) and see Pages your business owns (`business_management`). It never sees your Facebook password.

[Read how Profaily keeps your accounts safe](/blog/is-profaily-safe-to-connect-social-media-accounts).

## How your video goes out {#reels}

- **Up to 90 seconds, between landscape (16:9) and vertical (9:16):** published as a **Reel** on your Page.
- **Anything longer or wider:** published as a regular video on your Page.

When you schedule a video for Facebook, you can also:

- **Share to story:** adds the same video to your Page's story. Stories take videos up to 60 seconds.
- **Choose who sees it:** limit it to up to 25 countries, or set a minimum age (13+, 18+ or 21+).
- **Allow embedding** and **Allow other Pages to share it.**

Facebook Reels can't limit the audience or turn off embedding. Profaily tells you before you schedule if those settings won't apply.

## If it doesn't connect {#troubleshooting}

If Profaily shows one of these messages, here's what to do:

- **"Profaily needs permission to see and publish to your Facebook Pages."** Connect again and leave every permission switched on.
- **"No Facebook Pages on this account."** Create a Page, or ask a Page admin to give you a role on one, then connect again.
- **"Your role on this Facebook Page changed, so Profaily can no longer publish to it."** Ask a Page admin to give you the **Create content** task, then reconnect.
- **"Facebook didn't grant Profaily permission to publish to this Page."** Reconnect and leave every box checked.

## Frequently asked questions {#faq}

### Can I post to my personal Facebook profile?

No. Facebook only lets apps like Profaily publish to Pages, so Profaily connects the Pages you have a role on, not your personal profile.

### Does Profaily get my Facebook password?

No. You sign in on Facebook's own page, and Facebook gives Profaily permission to see your Pages and publish to them.
