You need two things: an Instagram **Business or Creator** account, and the **Admin** role in your Profaily team. If you have both, skip to [Connect Instagram in Profaily](#connect).

## Switch to a professional account {#switch}

Instagram only lets tools like Profaily post to professional accounts. A personal account can't be connected, and switching is free.

1. Open Instagram and go to your profile.
2. Tap the menu (three lines) at the top right.
3. Tap **Account type and tools**, then **Switch to professional account**.
4. Choose **Creator** or **Business** and follow the steps.

Instagram renames these menus from time to time. If you can't find them, search Instagram's settings for "professional account".

## Connect Instagram in Profaily {#connect}

1. In Profaily, open your account menu (your name, bottom left) and choose **Settings**. On a phone, open the menu (☰) first.
2. Tap **Brands**, then the **···** next to the brand you post for.
3. Tap **General**, then **Connect a channel**.
4. Next to Instagram, tap **Connect**.
5. Instagram opens. Sign in, check what Profaily is asking for, and approve it.
6. You come straight back to Profaily, and Instagram shows under **Channels**.

## What Profaily can do on Instagram {#permissions}

Profaily asks Instagram for two permissions: `instagram_business_basic` to see your basic account details, and `instagram_business_content_publish` to publish your videos. It never sees your Instagram password.

[Read how Profaily keeps your accounts safe](/blog/is-profaily-safe-to-connect-social-media-accounts).

## If it doesn't connect {#troubleshooting}

If Profaily shows one of these messages, here's what to do:

- **"Instagram needs a Business or Creator account to connect."** [Switch to a professional account](#switch), then connect again.
- **"Instagram didn't grant every permission Profaily needs."** Connect again and leave every permission switched on.
- **"Connection cancelled."** The Instagram window was closed before you approved the connection. Connect again.
- **"That took too long."** Connect again and finish within a few minutes.
- **"Instagram wasn't connected: your plan has room for no more channels."** Disconnect a channel you no longer use, or [change your plan](/pricing).
- **"Instagram isn't responding right now."** Instagram is having trouble. Try again in a few minutes.

## Instagram's posting rules {#rules}

Instagram checks every video before it publishes. Profaily tells you which rule a video broke, but it helps to know them up front:

- **Video format:** MP4 or MOV, encoded as H.264 with AAC audio.
- **Caption:** up to 2,200 characters and up to 20 @mentions.
- **No repeats:** Instagram rejects a video it sees as a duplicate of one you posted recently.
- **Daily limit:** each account can only publish so many posts a day through apps. If you reach it, try again the next day.

## Frequently asked questions {#faq}

### Can I connect a personal Instagram account?

No. Instagram only lets tools like Profaily post to professional accounts, meaning Business or Creator. Switching is free and takes a minute in Instagram's settings.

### Does Profaily get my Instagram password?

No. You sign in on Instagram's own page. Instagram then gives Profaily permission to publish and to read basic account details, nothing more.

### Who can connect an Instagram account?

Only an Admin of your Profaily team. Everyone else posts to the accounts an Admin has connected.
