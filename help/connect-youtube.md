You need the **Admin** role in your Profaily team, and a Google account that has a **YouTube channel**. If your Google account has no channel yet, create one on YouTube first.

## Connect YouTube in Profaily {#connect}

1. In Profaily, open your account menu (your name, bottom left) and choose **Settings**. On a phone, open the menu first.
2. Tap **Brands**, then the **···** next to the brand you post for.
3. Tap **General**, then **Connect a channel**.
4. Next to YouTube, tap **Connect**.
5. Google opens. Pick the account that owns your channel, check what Profaily is asking for, and approve it.
6. You come straight back to Profaily, and YouTube shows under **Channels**.

If your channel belongs to a Brand Account, Google may ask which channel to use. Pick the one you want Profaily to post to.

## What Profaily can do on YouTube {#permissions}

Profaily asks Google for three permissions:

- `openid` to confirm which Google account you connected.
- `youtube.upload` to upload your videos.
- `youtube.readonly` to read your channel details, such as its name.

It never sees your Google password. Google has verified Profaily's use of these permissions.

## If it doesn't connect {#troubleshooting}

If Profaily shows one of these messages, here's what to do:

- **"This Google account has no YouTube channel."** Create a channel on YouTube with that account, then connect again.
- **"YouTube didn't grant every permission Profaily needs."** Connect again and tick every permission Google shows.
- **"Connection cancelled."** The Google window was closed before you approved the connection. Connect again.
- **"YouTube wasn't connected: your plan has room for no more channels."** Disconnect a channel you no longer use, or [change your plan](/pricing).
- **"YouTube isn't responding right now."** YouTube is having trouble. Try again in a few minutes.

## YouTube's upload rules {#rules}

YouTube checks every video and its details before it publishes:

- **Format:** MP4, encoded as H.264 with AAC audio.
- **Title:** 1 to 100 characters, with no `<` or `>`.
- **Description:** up to 5,000 bytes (about 5,000 letters in English), with no `<` or `>`.
- **Tags:** all tags together must fit in 500 characters.
- **Daily limit:** each channel can only upload so many videos a day. If you reach it, try again in 24 hours.

To appear as a Short, a video should be vertical or square and no longer than three minutes. YouTube decides what counts as a Short.

## Frequently asked questions {#faq}

### Do I need a YouTube channel to connect?

Yes. Profaily publishes to a channel, so the Google account you sign in with needs one. Create it on YouTube first, then connect.

### Does Profaily get my Google password?

No. You sign in on Google's own page. Google gives Profaily permission to upload videos, read your channel details and confirm which account you connected.
