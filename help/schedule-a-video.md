Every video starts in the **composer**, the screen you see when you open Profaily. Here's the whole thing, recorded in the app, from upload to the Queue:

![Scheduling a video in Profaily: upload, write the caption, Publish, pick Tomorrow 09:00, Add to queue, then the video waits in the Queue](/assets/help/schedule-a-video/schedule-flow.mp4)

## Add your video {#add}

Drop a video into the composer, or tap it to choose a file. You can upload **MP4 or MOV videos, or images, up to 4 GB**. You can also **Import** a file or **Start with text**.

![The composer with a vertical video uploaded and its caption written, and a TikTok preview beside it](/assets/help/schedule-a-video/composer.jpg)

## Write your caption {#caption}

Write one caption and it's used for every platform. To change it for one platform only, tap that platform's icon at the top of the composer and edit it there. On YouTube, the first sentence of your caption is used as the video title.

![The YouTube tab, with Sync turned on, a Shorts preview and the note that the first sentence is used as the YouTube title](/assets/help/schedule-a-video/youtube-tab.jpg)

Each platform has its own limits. For example, Instagram captions can be up to 2,200 characters and YouTube titles up to 100.

## Choose where it goes {#platforms}

Tap **Publish** at the top of the composer. The **Schedule or publish** window opens and lists each connected account, each marked **Ready** when the video fits that platform's rules. Leave on the ones you want, and switch off any you don't.

If no account is listed, [connect one first](/help/topics/get-started).

![The Schedule or publish window, with the time box, Next slot, Pick a time, Publish now, and TikTok, Instagram and YouTube switched on and Ready](/assets/help/schedule-a-video/schedule-dialog.jpg)

## Pick when {#when}

- **Next slot** puts the video in your brand's next open posting time.
- **Pick a time** lets you choose the exact date and time.
- **Publish now** sends it straight away.

You can also tap the time box and type a time in plain words, like *tomorrow 9am* or *fri 17:00*, or tap one of your schedule slots.

![The time picker under the time box, with schedule slots for Tomorrow 09:00, Fri 20 Nov 17:00 and Mon 23 Nov 09:00 above a calendar](/assets/help/schedule-a-video/time-picker.jpg)

The big button changes to match. Tap it to confirm, for example **Add to queue · Tomorrow 09:00**.

![The Schedule or publish window with Thu 19 Nov, 09:00 chosen and the button reading Add to queue · Tomorrow 09:00](/assets/help/schedule-a-video/add-to-queue.jpg)

Not ready? Tap **Save draft** and finish it later.

If you're an **Editor**, you'll see **Send for review** instead. A Producer or Admin approves it before it goes out. [How approvals work](/help/approve-videos-before-they-post).

## After it's sent {#after}

Scheduled videos wait under **Queue**, grouped by day, and videos that went out are under **Posted**. They're the tabs above your list of videos. On a phone, open the menu (☰) first.

![The Queue tab with three scheduled videos grouped by day, the open slots with Use slot buttons, and a message saying Scheduled for Tomorrow 09:00](/assets/help/schedule-a-video/queue.jpg)
 If a platform refuses a video, it's marked **Failed** and you get an email with the reason. [Fix a failed post](/help/why-a-post-failed).

## Frequently asked questions {#faq}

### What files can I upload?

MP4 or MOV videos, or images, up to 4 GB each. Each platform then checks the video against its own rules before it posts.

### Can I save a video and finish it later?

Yes. Tap Save draft. Your drafts are under the Drafts tab.
