There are two ways to stop Profaily posting to an account, and you only need one.

## In Profaily {#in-profaily}

You need the **Admin** role in your Profaily team.

1. In Profaily, open the menu, tap your account, then **Settings**.
2. Tap **Brands**, then the **···** next to the brand.
3. Tap **General**. Your connected accounts are listed under **Channels**.
4. Tap the bin icon next to the account, then **Disconnect**.

![The Channels list in a brand's General settings, with a bin icon next to each connected account](/assets/help/remove-profaily-access/channels.jpg)

Profaily asks the platform to cancel its access, then deletes its own stored copy straight away. Asking the platform is best effort, so to be certain, also remove access on the platform.

## On the platform itself {#on-the-platform}

You don't need to sign in to Profaily for this.

- **YouTube:** go to your Google Account at [myaccount.google.com/connections](https://myaccount.google.com/connections), choose Profaily, and remove its access.
- **TikTok and Instagram:** open the app's settings, find the list of apps and websites that have access to your account, and remove Profaily. The menu names change from time to time, so the exact path may differ on your phone.

Once you remove it there, the platform has cancelled Profaily's access. To post again, you'd have to reconnect on purpose.

## Delete your Profaily account {#delete-account}

To remove everything at once, open **Settings → Your user → Delete account**. It takes effect immediately: Profaily disconnects every account, cancels scheduled videos, deletes your uploads and removes your data.

You can also email [privacy@useprofaily.com](mailto:privacy@useprofaily.com) and ask us to do it.

## Frequently asked questions {#faq}

### Do I need to do both?

No, either one stops Profaily posting. Removing access on the platform itself is the surest way, because it doesn't depend on Profaily at all.

### What happens to my scheduled posts?

If you disconnect in Profaily, that account is taken off your scheduled videos, and they still go out to your other accounts. If you remove access on the platform instead, that account's posts wait until you reconnect it.

### Can I get my access back later?

Yes. Connect the account again from Settings → Brands → General → Connect a channel.
