The **Business** plan comes with a **14-day free trial**. You add a card to start it, and you're not charged until the trial ends.

## Start the trial {#start}

1. Go to [useprofaily.com](/) and tap **Sign up**.
2. Sign in with Google, TikTok or Instagram.
3. Choose **Business** and add your card.
4. Connect your accounts and post your first video.

![The Profaily sign-in screen with Continue with Google, and TikTok and Instagram buttons](/assets/help/start-free-trial/auth-login.jpg)

Once you're in, Profaily asks you to connect your first account. Tap **Connect** next to TikTok, Instagram or YouTube. You can add the rest later.

![The Connect your first account screen, with Connect buttons for TikTok, Instagram and YouTube](/assets/help/start-free-trial/g-gate.jpg)

## During the trial {#during}

You get everything in Business for 14 days: 3 brands, 12 connected channels, 5 team members, and approvals before videos go out.

A good way to try it: connect a test or secondary account first, schedule a few videos, and watch them go out. Connect your main accounts once you're happy.

You can see when your trial ends in **Settings → Billing**, under your plan.

## When the trial ends {#ends}

- **If you keep going**, your card is charged for the plan you chose, and everything carries on.
- **If you cancel before the trial ends**, you aren't charged. Cancel any time from **Settings → Billing → Cancel subscription**. [How to cancel](/help/change-or-cancel-your-plan).

## Frequently asked questions {#faq}

### Do I need a card to start the trial?

Yes. You add a card to start, and you're not charged until the 14 days are over. Cancel before then and you pay nothing.

### Does the Creator plan have a trial?

No. The free trial is on the Business plan. You can move to Creator at any time from Settings → Billing.
