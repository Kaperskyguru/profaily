Everything about your plan is in **Settings → Billing**. In a team, the person who owns the team manages billing.

## Change your plan {#change}

1. In Profaily, open the menu, tap your account, then **Settings**.
2. Tap **Billing**. Your plan, your next payment and how much of your plan you're using are at the top.

![Settings, Billing, showing Business Monthly at ₦19,900 a month, the next payment, and plan usage for brands, channels and seats](/assets/help/change-or-cancel-your-plan/bl-billing.jpg)

3. Tap **View all plans**.
4. Pick **Creator** or **Business**. Turn on **Yearly billing** to pay yearly and save 20%.

![The Upgrade or Change Plan window, with the Yearly billing switch and the Creator plan's Downgrade button](/assets/help/change-or-cancel-your-plan/bl-plans.jpg)

Profaily shows you what changes and what you'll pay before you confirm.

## Update your card {#card}

Go to **Settings → Billing → Update payment method**.

## Cancel {#cancel}

1. Go to **Settings → Billing**.
2. Tap **Cancel subscription**. Profaily tells you how long you keep your plan, then confirm with **Cancel plan**, or tap **Keep plan** if you've changed your mind.

![The cancel message: You keep Business until 30 Nov, then your account is read-only. Nothing is deleted. With Keep plan and Cancel plan buttons](/assets/help/change-or-cancel-your-plan/bl-cancel2.jpg)

You keep everything until the end of the period you've paid for, or the end of your trial, and you won't be charged again. Changed your mind? Tap **Resume subscription** before then.

Cancelling keeps your account. To remove your account and data too, [delete your account](/help/delete-your-account).

If something made Profaily not work for you, we'd really like to know. Email [support@useprofaily.com](mailto:support@useprofaily.com).

## Frequently asked questions {#faq}

### Will I be charged after I cancel?

No. You keep using Profaily until the end of the period you've paid for, or the end of your trial, and you aren't charged again.

### Can I undo a cancellation?

Yes. Until your plan ends, go to Settings → Billing and tap Resume subscription.
